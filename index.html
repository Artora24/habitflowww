Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how HabitFlow ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our habit-tracking application. This policy is provided as a template — please replace it with your own legal text before launching a real product.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Usage data: the habits you create, completion history, streaks, and activity within the app.
  • Device data: browser type, operating system, and general usage analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide and improve the service, personalize your experience, send optional reminders and summary emails you've opted into, and maintain the security of our platform.

4. Local Storage

Your habits, settings, and profile are stored locally in your browser (via localStorage) and are never transmitted to a server. Clearing your browser data will remove this information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers who help us operate the app (such as email delivery or analytics providers), and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those services.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can reset your data at any time from Settings → Danger Zone, or contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the "last updated" date above.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out at privacy@habitflow.app.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember information about your visit. HabitFlow also uses localStorage, a similar browser storage mechanism, to remember your preferences.

2. How We Use Cookies & Local Storage

  • Essential: remembering that you're logged in during your session.
  • Preferences: remembering your chosen theme (light, dark, or system) and notification settings.
  • Product data: storing the habits, streaks, and activity you create, entirely on your device.

All of the above is stored locally in your browser only — nothing is sent to a third-party analytics or advertising service.

3. Third-Party Cookies

A production version of HabitFlow may use third-party cookies for analytics (to understand how the product is used) or customer support tools. We would list any such providers here.

4. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and local storage through their settings. Note that disabling storage will prevent HabitFlow from remembering your habits, streaks, and theme preference between visits.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in the tools we use. Updates will be posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

Questions about our use of cookies can be sent to privacy@habitflow.app.
